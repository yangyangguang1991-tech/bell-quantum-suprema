original authorship and required notices are retained. Other retained library
components preserve their own notices and licensing terms. PowerShell/.NET and
MSVC runtime binaries are not bundled. Windows and CPython, including their
platform runtime components, remain prerequisites. Historical papers are cited
for scholarly attribution; their texts are not licensed by this repository's
MIT license.
