# Mathematical contributions and prior results

This map assigns a contribution description to all 228 catalogue positions in this repository. Six bipartite classes occur in both component catalogues. Repeated positions are not additional mathematical discoveries. All historical inequalities, labels and facet lists retain their original authorship.

The contribution of this edition is its supplied mathematical exposition, exact certificate data, verification implementations, explicit attainment or approximation constructions, and coefficient correspondences. An independently supplied exact proof, a previously published formula, and the first discovery of a value are different claims. This edition does not claim worldwide first discovery for an entry merely because the literature review did not identify an earlier exact proof.

The source comparison uses the coefficient normalization and quantum model specified in each entry. It concerns the sources identified in the historical notes, including the recent literature reviewed for this edition; it cannot exclude an inaccessible or differently indexed earlier proof.

## Meaning of the contribution categories

- **Earlier exact theorem:** an earlier strict result, or an explicitly identified consequence of one, gives the value. This edition reproduces or specializes it and supplies its own presentation or verification materials; the value is not claimed as new.
- **Earlier analytic expression; exact certificate supplied here:** the same expression was already reported. Credit for that expression remains with the earlier authors. The contribution asserted here is the supplied exact proof and reproducible certificate, not discovery of that expression. No claim that this is its first rigorous proof is made.
- **Exact proof materials supplied here; earlier numerical work credited:** this edition supplies the exact global argument and attainment data. Earlier numerical values, constructions and methods retain credit. An earlier exact theorem was not identified in the reviewed sources; this is not a determination of global priority.
- **Elementary consequence:** positivity or another stated elementary implication. No new-value claim.
- **Unknown:** no exact unrestricted quantum supremum is asserted by this edition.

## What is and is not claimed as new

The specific contribution claimed for this repository edition is the supplied proof and certificate collection, its implementations and exposition, and its reproducible integration. The historical facet catalogues, identified earlier strict theorems and already reported analytical expressions are not claimed as newly discovered results. The entries described as exact proof materials supplied here identify where this edition contributes a complete exact argument without assigning worldwide first-proof priority.

There is no certified count of globally first-discovered quantum values in this release. Such a claim would require a separate, statement-by-statement priority argument beyond the evidence of a successful proof or a search without a prior match. In particular, the 228 positions, the 224 closed positions, and the certificate counts must not be advertised as numbers of new theorems.
