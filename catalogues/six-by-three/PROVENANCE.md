# Mathematical provenance

## Seven representatives and external mathematical results

The complete scenario classification is credited to Jesus and Zambrini
Cruzeiro, *Tight Bell inequalities from polytope slices*, Physical Review A
108, 052220 (2023). The local circuit enumeration in `common/` and the
completeness materials are project implementations. The seven indexed
representatives retain the probability-positivity, CHSH, Collins--Gisin
I3322, Brunner--Gisin I4322 and I5322 attributions recorded in
[HISTORY.md](HISTORY.md). A classification source is not presented as a
strict proof of a quantum value.

CHSH's exact unrestricted value is credited to Cirel'son. The I3322 proof
exposition and path-sequence implementation acknowledge Coladangelo's
path/Bellman construction and Pauwels' spectral comparison. The
[I3322 bibliography](entries/02-I3322/REFERENCES.md) distinguishes their
claims and the separate later characterizations. These are mathematical
sources for the argument; the project does not claim to have originated
those theorems. No external Lean development, article source, author code
repository or downloaded certificate is required by the included verifier.

## Exact code and data derivations

The former description "earlier Bell Exact Bounds Project" referred to the
user-organized predecessor materials, not to an identified third-party
software distribution. The two arithmetic derivations are pinned explicitly
in [PROVENANCE.json](PROVENANCE.json): `common/positive.py` adapts the
predecessor `code/interval_ldl.py`, while `common/quadratic.py` adapts
`code/exact_core.py`. The version-19 input hashes and current output hashes
are recorded, including the interface and assertion changes.

The Gram inputs for I4322^(1) and I4322^(2) retain exact selected fields from
the predecessor exact SOS JSON certificates. Every retained field was
compared directly with its source. The strategy construction program
creates the displayed Pauli matrices and normalized state in exact radical
arithmetic. The I4322^(3) and I5322 operator arguments, local normalization
and attainment calculations are retained as project expositions and
implementations; the historical definition and numerical papers are not
credited as prior strict proofs without evidence of such a proof.

All seven entry groups and shared mathematical files are tied to the
pre-consolidation constituent edition by SHA256. This catalogue uses the
Python standard library; an acknowledgement of a mathematical method is
distinct from redistribution of a third-party software implementation.

## Interpretation of the provenance record

[PROVENANCE.json](PROVENANCE.json) identifies each numbered entry, the exact
SHA256 of its supplied files, the pre-consolidation constituent edition, and
the content identities of the extraction/adaptation programs. A tree digest
is defined there by an explicit canonical JSON encoding. The historical
builder identities document transfer history; those private build programs
are not required to verify any distributed proof.

Yang-Guang Yang has confirmed that he organized the predecessor project
materials and can license the project's own new code, prose and certificates,
with no undisclosed rights holders or restrictions. This is the contributor's
declaration, not an independent legal adjudication. Identified third-party
software, publications, mathematical catalogues and their original notices
remain separate. In older project artifacts, the generic label "Bell Exact
Bounds Project contributors" denoted that predecessor collection; it is not
used here as evidence for an unidentified external software project's license.

The records distinguish a published mathematical definition, a previously
proved theorem, and this distribution's implementation or certificate.
Reproducing a proof does not transfer priority for the theorem. Conversely,
a historical numerical value or a formula accepted by numerical agreement
is not relabelled as an earlier strict unrestricted proof. Prior strict-value
citations are identified within the relevant entry; the wider history includes
classification, numerical work and general methods.

The numbered entries contain the Bell coefficients, exact root selectors,
positivity inputs, physical strategies and verification programs. No claim
of worldwide priority or independent invention follows from a file hash.
