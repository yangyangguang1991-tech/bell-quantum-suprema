# Completeness of the six-versus-three catalogue

The classification agrees with Jesus and Cruzeiro (2023), cited in [HISTORY.md](HISTORY.md). Here is an independent, finite verification, including the positivity facet. It uses only integer arithmetic and the eight vertices of Bob's three-dimensional binary cube. Run `python -B common/classification.py`.

## Generators of the cone of valid inequalities

Every affine Bell functional on deterministic assignments can be written as
$$
g(b)+\sum_{i=1}^6 a_i r_i(b),\qquad a_i\in\{0,1\},\quad b\in\{0,1\}^3,
$$
where $g$ and every $r_i$ are affine functions of $b$. The inequality is valid with right side zero precisely if $g(b)+\sum_i (r_i(b))_+\le0$ at all eight vertices. Keep the signs of one row at these eight points fixed. The resulting closed polyhedral cone of row coefficients is pointed because the eight evaluation forms span the dual of the four-dimensional affine-function space. Its extreme rays are obtained from triples of independent evaluation planes by taking a null vector in either direction. A lower-dimensional sign chamber lies in the closure of a full-dimensional one. Thus a row is a nonnegative combination of finitely many listed ray rows in the same closed chamber. The positive part is additive on that combination at every Bob vertex, including vertices where the original row vanishes.

For a ray $r$, attach the eight-vector $h=r_+$. Add eight slack rays with $h$ a coordinate unit vector and no Alice row. The four Fourier characters on Bob subsets of size two and three detect exactly whether an eight-vector is affine. Write $D(h)$ for its four coefficients. A valid functional decomposes into ray terms with coefficients $x\ge0$ satisfying $\sum xD(h)=0$. The affine part $g$ is then exactly $-\sum xh$ on the eight vertices. Conversely every such positive dependence gives a valid Bell inequality: the ray $r$ may be assigned to any Alice setting, and $a_i r(b)\le h(b)$ holds pointwise. The original row decomposition retains each ray's Alice setting.

The cone of nonnegative dependencies is generated by its extreme rays. On the support of an extreme ray the kernel must be one-dimensional, since a second independent dependency would permit a two-sided perturbation of its positive coefficients. Thus each generator has at most five terms and is a minimal positive dependence, or is a singleton zero column. A dependence with two positively proportional columns cannot be extreme. The program groups proportional columns after a positive rescaling and reinstates all scalar factors when building the table. There can be several possible Alice assignments of the ray rows: it enumerates all partitions into equal-setting groups. Labels of those groups are irrelevant up to input permutations. At most five of the six available settings are required.

The local polytope is full-dimensional in the 27 probability coordinates, since the constant, all single bits and all cross-products are linearly independent functions on the deterministic cube. A facet inequality therefore generates an extreme ray of the cone of valid affine inequalities. If it is a sum of the valid generators above, each nonzero summand must be a positive multiple of that facet. This proves that testing the entire generator list suffices for exhaustiveness.

## Finite checks

The program forms 40 signed affine ray rows and eight slack atoms. The quotient contains 14 distinct nonzero column directions. It enumerates positive dependencies with two to five terms by checking their signed minors and multiplying the proposed null vector by the original columns. Bob permutations and output flips act on the four Fourier characters by signed permutations. Only equivalent labelings are removed. Canonical forms also include the constant and every marginal; Alice row permutations and Alice output flips are removed in reflection coordinates.

For each candidate table, all saturating deterministic assignments over a fixed Bob vertex are spanned by one base assignment and the differences obtained by flipping rows with zero value. Exact integer row reduction then tests the homogeneous rank of these assignments. Rejecting a nonfacet before padding Alice is sound: an extra Alice setting can increase this rank by at most four, exactly the increase in ambient dimension. A Bob-only candidate is tested with an explicit Alice row first. For the seven retained tables, `common/census.py` independently verifies rank 27 in the fully padded scenario and the local bound on all 512 assignments.

There are 2,431 distinct candidate tables after the first symmetry reduction and 98 candidate orbits. Exactly seven coincide with facets: joint positivity, CHSH, $I_{3322}$, $I_{4322}^{(1)}$, $I_{4322}^{(2)}$, $I_{4322}^{(3)}$, and $I_{5322}$.
