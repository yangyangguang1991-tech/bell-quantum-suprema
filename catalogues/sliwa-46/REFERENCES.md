## Recent methods and computational contributions

- L. Coccia, M. Padovan and G. Vallone, *Systematic derivation of Tsirelson bounds in arbitrary dimensions* (2026). Their construction concerns bipartite SOS bounds. It provides methodological context for global certification. [arXiv:2606.21626](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.21626).
- Percivio Ltd., *Certified Science*, public verification repository (consulted 5 November 2026). Its authors report AI-assisted witness searches and distinguish them from independent exact verification. The J62 artifact concerns a bipartite certified interval. It is cited as a related computational contribution; all 46 tripartite proof inputs used here are distributed locally. [Project](https://github.com/Percivio-Ltd/certified-science).

### Party lifts

D. Rosset, J.-D. Bancal and N. Gisin, *Classifying 50 years of Bell inequalities*, Journal of Physics A: Mathematical and Theoretical **47**, 424022 (2014). Section II.4 and Appendix C.2, Proposition 4 give tight bounds for composite inequalities. Substituting the factors $I-A$ and $H-2I$, with $H$ the CHSH operator, proves the S004 value $4\sqrt{2}-2$ and its attainability. This is a specialization of their general theorem, not a claim that the paper explicitly labels S004. [Article](https://doi.org/10.1088/1751-8113/47/42/424022); [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.1306v1).

C. Jebarathinam, J.-C. Hung, S.-L. Chen and Y.-C. Liang, *Maximal violation of a broad class of Bell inequalities and its implication on self-testing*, Physical Review Research **1**, 033073 (2019). Section III.C, Observation 1 proves that a party lift preserves the maximal quantum violation in zero-local-bound normalization. The S004 note gives the conversion to the correlator normalization used here. [Article](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.1.033073).

### Exact tripartite full-correlation bounds

E. Panwar, P. Pandya and M. Wieśniak, *An elegant scheme of self-testing for multipartite Bell inequalities*, npj Quantum Information **9**, 71 (2023). Theorem 6 and the tripartite WWWŻB discussion cover the Mermin and extended CHSH forms. Equation (27) and its adjoining text give the exact value $20/3$ for the literal seventh Śliwa inequality. [Article](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41534-023-00735-3).
