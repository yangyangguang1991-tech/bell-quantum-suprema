# Scientific overview

## Abstract

This collection provides exact global upper bounds and explicit attaining strategies for the 46 Śliwa facet representatives in the tripartite two-setting two-outcome Bell scenario. The fixed correlator normalization is preserved throughout. Rational or radical expressions specify 28 entries; eight values are isolated univariate roots, and ten are coordinates of uniquely isolated real solutions of polynomial systems. The exact upper proofs combine two-reflection dimension reduction with operator identities, characteristic-polynomial positivity, rational Bernstein covers and interval localization. A finite arithmetic construction supplies a normalized attaining three-qubit state for every entry. Historical values and exact prior certificates are credited separately from the present executable proof corpus.

## Problem and main result

Write the observables as $A,a;B,b;C,c$. Outcomes are $\pm1$. The Bell functional $\mathcal B_i$ is the literal expression in `entries/Sxxx/functional.json`, with its stated local bound. Its terms contain at most one measurement from each party. There is no assumption that a party's two measurements commute.

Let $Q_{\rm fin}$ denote the supremum over arbitrary finite-dimensional tensor-product strategies, $Q_{\rm sp}$ the spatial supremum, and $Q_{\rm com}$ the supremum over commuting representations. For every $i=1,\ldots,46$ the supplied certificates and strategies establish

$$Q_{\rm fin}(\mathcal B_i)=Q_{\rm sp}(\mathcal B_i)=Q_{\rm com}(\mathcal B_i)=q_i.$$

Each $q_i$ is attained by an explicitly specified real three-qubit pure state. Binary POVMs are included by a common local dilation. The three exact-value formats account for 28, eight and ten catalogue entries, respectively; repeated values can occur in different entries.

The complete literal expressions, exact parameter definitions and normalized states are in the numbered entries. The bundled reference tables include the full catalogue, polynomial definitions and compact selecting boxes. `data/compact_root_selectors.json` gives the corresponding rational certificate data; `tools/verify_root_selectors.py` rechecks all 18 non-radical selectors independently of decimal root finding.

## Historical context and contributions

Śliwa's arXiv appendix fixes the representatives; Pitowsky and Svozil supplied an earlier facet enumeration. Masanes established qubit attainability for extreme correlations in the two-dichotomic-observable scenario. These structural results are prior work.

The 2016 benchmark of López-Rosa, Xu and Cabello reports 27 analytical entries and 19 decimal entries in Section II A and Table IV. All 27 analytical entries agree symbolically with the present expressions. Sixteen of the 19 older decimal displays are incompatible with rounding the present exact values to the printed precision. All 46 present values agree with the quantum column `Q` of Vallins, Sainz and Liang, Table I on the fifth physical PDF page of corrected arXiv version 5 (18 July 2018). These are comparisons with reported values, not claims that older numerical tables supplied exact global bounds.

Werner and Wolf's full-correlation classification includes a representative equivalent to S7: flipping the first-setting output at all three parties and multiplying their expression by four maps the normalized reported value `5/3` to `20/3` (Appendix, Eq. (A2)). Their table is described as numerical and is not used here as an exact upper certificate. Ioannou and Rosset give four explicit class-level SOS certificates in Section IX, Eqs. (156), (158), (160) and (162), for classes 3, 10, 11 and 14, respectively. The precise attribution, normalization and proof scope for every entry are recorded in [Prior work and contributions](PRIOR_WORK_AND_CONTRIBUTIONS.md), with full citations in [References](../REFERENCES.md).

The present contribution is the complete exact certificate corpus in one literal normalization and the constructive, independently checked attaining-state distribution. In particular, the nineteen rows printed numerically in the 2016 benchmark receive exact definitions and global certificates here. This is a benchmark-specific contribution statement, not an assertion that the underlying nineteen values had never previously been identified in any form.

Panwar, Pandya and Wieśniak (2023) give an exact global calculation for class 7 and also treat the Mermin and extended CHSH full-correlation forms (classes 2 and 3). [Article](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41534-023-00735-3).

## Global reduction

For two local reflections $R,S$, the overlap $T=(RS+SR)/2$ is central in the algebra generated by the parties. Joint spectral decomposition of the three overlaps gives scalars $x,y,z\in[-1,1]$. In an interior fibre,

$$R=Z\otimes I,\qquad S=(xZ+\sqrt{1-x^2}\,X)\otimes I.$$

The square root is nonnegative. The commutant of these two matrices acts only on the multiplicity space, so the argument repeats for all three parties. At an endpoint $x=\pm1$, the square root is zero, $S=\pm R$ and the deterministic branches are included. Thus a cube-wide bound for the canonical real symmetric $8\times8$ Bell matrix covers every commuting representation. The proof also handles direct integrals and cyclic reductions of nonseparable representations. Details are in [Global quantum bounds and attaining strategies](PROOF_GUIDE.md).

For a binary POVM, its observable is a self-adjoint contraction $T$. On a common local dilation space use the reflection

$$\widehat T=\begin{pmatrix}T&\sqrt{I-T^2}\\\sqrt{I-T^2}&-T\end{pmatrix}.$$

The same embedding works for both settings. Separate ancillary factors preserve cross-party commutation and every Bell correlator.

## Exact global positivity

Let $M_i(x,y,z)$ be the canonical matrix and $P_i(q,x,y,z)=\det(qI-M_i)$. The literal characteristic polynomial is reconstructed exactly over $\mathbb Q[x,y,z]$ before it is used. Writing

$$P_i(q_i+t,x,y,z)=t^8+e_1t^7+\cdots+e_8,$$

nonnegativity of every $e_j$ implies $q_iI-M_i\succeq0$: a negative eigenvalue would give a positive root of a polynomial whose leading coefficient is one and remaining coefficients are nonnegative.

The certificates check the complete family of required positivity obligations, recompute Bernstein coefficients on rational boxes and prove complete box coverage. Each verifier applies its format's obligation and subdivision checks; selected formats additionally require unique records and leaf paths. Near a contact point, an exact zero and vanishing gradient combine with a positive semidefinite Hessian bound and Taylor's integral formula. Algebraic coefficients are enclosed by outward rational intervals. S16 and S46 additionally use the boundary and factorization reductions stated in their entry-specific proof notes. Simple entries admit direct reflection identities. A failed numerical search or a close decimal match plays no role in the exact acceptance conditions.

## Root localization

Univariate definitions include an exact root count and selecting interval. For polynomial systems $F=0$, choose a rational centre $a$ and a rational preconditioner $C$ for the Jacobian near $a$. An interval enclosure of $I-CF'(X)$ with norm below one, together with strict inclusion of the Newton image in $X$, proves existence and uniqueness by the contraction theorem. The compact selectors used in the manuscript enclose the original certified boxes and select the same roots. Their derivation uses interval analysis [Krawczyk1969; Moore2009].

## Exact constructive attainment

At the selected legal overlaps write $P_i(t)=(t-q_i)^mR_i(t)$, where $m$ is the exact multiplicity and $R_i(q_i)\ne0$. The spectral theorem gives $R_i(M_i)=R_i(q_i)\Pi_{q_i}$. The supplied fixed column index $j$ has a certified positive squared norm, so

$$\psi_i=\frac{R_i(M_i)e_j}{\sqrt{\|R_i(M_i)e_j\|^2}}$$

is explicit, normalized and satisfies $M_i\psi_i=q_i\psi_i$. The denominator is the strictly positive square root. The exact state files list a finite arithmetic graph for every amplitude. Eight real amplitudes and six measurement matrices are also supplied at 80 digits. Independent Decimal checks reconstruct the Bell score, the matrix score and the eigen-equation; a separate evaluator checks the amplitude graph. The numerical file's displayed eigen-equation residual is evaluated from its published rounded state, observables and value, with the evaluation scope and working precision recorded. These decimal checks supplement the exact proof.

## Verification and interpretation

A complete mathematical replay checks 46 upper-and-attainment chains and a fixed schedule of 52 upper-verifier invocations. The verification driver binds the submitted configuration to this schedule and checks the unchanged contents and target bindings of the inputs actually executed, with derived reports assigned separate output roles. Its S40 interval checks and S46 boundary verifier preserve the same exact target across the global upper proof and attainment. The defining values, literal expressions and attaining states are retained. No SDP solver or network access is required for replay.

Exactness here means a selected real algebraic number with a global proof and legal attainment. A defining polynomial is not asserted to be minimal unless separately established. The construction does not assert self-testing or uniqueness of the optimal strategy. The software is an inspectable computational proof development rather than a formal proof-kernel development.
